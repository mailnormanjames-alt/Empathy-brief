About

A studio, not a vendor.

Empathy is a Mumbai-based, emotion-led design studio built for brands, founders, and filmmakers who need to be felt before they're understood. Founded by Norman James, the studio treats every engagement as a directed piece of communication — never a template, never a formula.

Work spans brand identity, cinematic web design and development, product UI/UX, and 3D & motion — for clients across independent film (Bakery Films) and premium brand launches in the Indian market, with growing reach into international briefs.
