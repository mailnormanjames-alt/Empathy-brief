About

A studio, not a vendor.

Empathy is a Mumbai-based, emotion-led design studio built for brands, founders, and filmmakers who need to be felt before they're understood. Founded by Norman James, the studio treats every engagement as a directed piece of communication — never a template, never a formula.

Work spans brand identity, cinematic web design and development, product UI/UX, and 3D & motion — for clients across independent film (Bakery Films) and premium brand launches in the Indian market, with growing reach into international briefs.

Why now

The market is ready for direction.

DEMAND
Indian brand and founder spend on premium identity and digital presence is rising faster than the supply of studios that can direct it end-to-end.
POSITION
Empathy sits at the intersection of brand, web, product, and motion — most competitors specialise in one and outsource the rest, losing the thread.
LEVERAGE
An AI-native creative workflow lets a small studio work at the pace and polish of a much larger one, without diluting craft.
TIMING
Global clients are actively looking for Indian creative talent that meets international craft standards — the door is open now.
